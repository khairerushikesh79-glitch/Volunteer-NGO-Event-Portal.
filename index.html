About Our Portal

VolunteerConnect is a community volunteer management portal designed to connect people who want to volunteer with NGOs and community service opportunities.

Our platform makes it easier to discover volunteering opportunities, register for events and participate in meaningful social activities.

How It Works

1. Register

Create your volunteer or NGO account.

2. Find Opportunities

Explore volunteering events and community activities.

3. Join an Event

Enroll in an event that matches your interests.

4. Make an Impact

Participate and contribute to your community.
